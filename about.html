Our Story

From a Home Kitchen to Your Heart

Amore Cake started in 2018 in a small home kitchen in Pune. What began as weekend baking for friends and family quickly turned into a labour of love — driven by the joy that a perfect cake can bring to any occasion.

The word Amore means love in Italian, and that is exactly what goes into every creation we make. From selecting the freshest ingredients to hand-painting each detail, every cake is a work of edible art.

Today, Amore Cake serves hundreds of happy customers across Pune, creating memories one slice at a time.
